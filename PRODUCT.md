# BA4L

Big Al’s 4 Life is a four-person bowling league team app for Doug, Mustafa, Kyle and Pete. Web and native SwiftUI share account identity, scorebooks and league APIs. Primary jobs: record the next roll quickly, follow team/match results, browse season history and records, review a night with Bowling Bro’. Preserve shared-data authorization, account-scoped offline backups, conflicts, scan/voice review and official standings provenance. Never fabricate dates, standings, scores, quotes, mascot history or founding year.
