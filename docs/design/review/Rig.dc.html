THE RIG · NO HARDWARE TO BUY

One iPad on the floor, behind the foul line.

Top-down view of a lane pair. The iPad leans on the ball return, or on a $12 tablet stand on the approach floor, pointed down the lane. Its rear camera sees your lane, all 60 feet; the iOS app does the tracking on-device and posts each shot to the team scorebook.

WHAT THE iPAD DOES
Watches for a ball crossing the foul line; starts a 4 s clip. Tracks the ball at 60 fps (Vision framework, on-device). Speed from foul line to pins. Break point from the curve. Freeze frame at pin contact: entry angle, pins standing. Posts the shot to the scorebook; the phones update. Missed or double-counted a shot: one tap to redo, never a form.
CALIBRATION · ONCE PER NIGHT

Tap the foul line, the arrows, and the headpin on screen. Three taps and every measurement is in feet and boards, not pixels.

HONEST LIMITS

Speed ±0.3 mph, break ±2 boards, from a floor-level camera. Good enough for “you were slow tonight.” Not a LaneTrax. Say so in the app.
